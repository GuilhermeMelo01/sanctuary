### 1. Daily 150-word story

Write a short story every day using **5 required words**.

Example:  
**Topic:** A day when everything went wrong  
**Words:** suddenly, mistake, explain, nervous, improve

This helps grammar, vocabulary, and fluency.

### 2. Rewrite the same text 3 times

Write one paragraph, then rewrite it:

First version: simple English  
Second version: more detailed  
Third version: more natural and fluent

Example:

Simple:

> I went to work. I was tired.

More fluent:

> I went to work feeling exhausted, but I tried to stay focused.

### 3. Sentence expansion

Start with a basic sentence and make it richer.

Basic:

> I studied English.

Expanded:

> I studied English for one hour after work because I wanted to improve my writing and feel more confident.

This is great for learning connectors like **because, although, however, while, since**.

### 4. Imitation exercise

Take a sentence from a book, article, or series and copy the structure.

Original:

> Although it was difficult, I decided to continue.

Your version:

> Although I was tired, I decided to study.

This helps you sound more natural.

### 5. Error journal

Every time I correct your text, save your mistakes in categories:

| Mistake                      | Correction                  | Rule               |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------- | ------------------ |
| I went to my job             | I went to work              | Natural expression |
| focusing in my studies       | focusing on my studies      | Preposition        |
| I have a great night’s sleep | I had a great night’s sleep | Past tense         |

Review this before writing the next text.

### 6. One grammar focus per text

Don’t try to practice everything at once. Choose one grammar target.

For example:

Monday: past simple  
Tuesday: present perfect  
Wednesday: conditionals  
Thursday: connectors  
Friday: reported speech
